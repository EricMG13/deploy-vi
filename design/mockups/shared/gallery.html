What the three Report mockups share. Caesars figures are real and cited; every score, call and thesis line is illustrative, because no RBOT run exists.

Colour

House pair navy and electric blue on a cool grey ground. Mint and lemon are accents for state on navy only. The chart palette is five hues, a neutral and a critical red; blue against orange stays distinct under every common colour-vision deficiency, and lines also differ by point shape and dash.

      Charts

      Every chart has a title, a one-line summary, a legend and a table twin. Each mark is focusable (Tab), names its value and origin, and opens its source on Enter or click. Solid marks are cited or calculated; hatched, hollow or dashed marks are projected, model-authored or illustrative; an n/a gap carries its reason.
