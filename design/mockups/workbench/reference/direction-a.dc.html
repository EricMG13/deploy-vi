A1Statement: thesis, colour, type, components, risk
Thesis
A left rail of destinations grouped by analysis family, a call band that stays pinned while you scroll, and every block as a self-describing card on a calm grid: the shape PMs already know from analytics tools.
Honest risk
Cards cost vertical space. Report A fit four chart plots and the call in 900px; A fits them too, but with 24px less table height per block, and the family grouping adds seven headings to a 17-module rail. The rail also competes with 1280px laptops: it must collapse to icons there by default.

Component language
The call: a white band with six scale badges; the position box takes the signal colour, the word sits beside it, a five-step meter after it. Pinned on scroll; compacts to one row.
Blocks: cards with a 6px radius, a header naming owner module and state, a footer with source line and "Full analysis".
Tables: 13px, hairline rules, numbers right in tabular figures; in-cell headroom bars in CSS.
Charts: SVG, issuer blue against burnt orange, gaps break the line and print n/a, every mark focusable.
Status and verdict: icon + word chips (tick, half circle, slashed circle, warning, cross) on a tinted fill.
Provenance (never a signal colour): cited blue corner triangle; calculated navy square; synthetic hollow grey diamond; model-written: dotted underline and a pen tag; illustrative: dashed outline tag; gap: n/a G8.
Icons: one 16px stroke set at 1.5px: statuses, verdicts, directions, severity bars, families in the rail, actions. Words stay on scales, numbers, headings and gap reasons.

How it holds 17. Family headings (eight for the full scope) group the modules so the rail reads as eight decisions, not seventeen rows; module rows are 22px and family headings 16px, so 17 modules plus the fixed destinations fit in 900px with the header; search becomes a button. The collapsed rail crops the supplied logo to its mark (never redrawn). Status is an icon per row (tick, half circle, slashed circle), the family dot in the collapsed rail shows the worst status inside; hovering or focusing a family opens its flyout. Below 1280px the rail starts collapsed. Portfolio Fit shows its verdict; Evidence and Limitations show their counts. Pathways with 6–9 modules use the same rail without family headings.
