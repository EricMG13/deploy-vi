BDirection B: Reading page. Top bar and tabs, a docked verdict ribbon, one ruled reading column with a right-hand dock for outline and sources.
B1Statement: thesis, colour, type, components, risk
Thesis
Navigation lives in a top bar, so the whole width belongs to one reading column; the verdict is a ribbon that docks under the tabs and travels with you; sources open in a side dock, not over the text.
Honest risk
A top bar holds six tabs well and seventeen modules badly: the Analysis tab must open a module strip, one extra click to any module. The serif heading voice is the most "paper" of the three; if the owner reads it as a committee document rather than a product, the ribbon and dock will not save it.
Colour system
Navy #000533 top bar, ribbon edge, ink · 19.6:1
Electric blue #0017E5 links, active tab, cited underline, issuer series · 9.3:1
Paper #FAFAFC, rules #E4E6EE, secondary ink #5B6170 · 6.2:1
Positive teal #0B7A6E 5.2:1 on white; outlined chips, no fill
Caution ochre #8F5600 6.0:1 on white
Negative crimson #B42318 6.6:1 on white; white on it 6.6:1
Chart comparison #C4410C 5.1:1; signal colours appear in charts only at thresholds
Type system
Instrument Serif
Titles and section heads only, 22–30px, regular weight. OFL.
Instrument Sans
Everything else: body 14/400, tables 13 with tabular numerals, labels 12/500, call words 16/600. OFL.
Section 22 serifBody 14/400 · 1.55Table 13 · 11,648 · 3.29xLabel 12/500
Component language
The call: a ribbon under the tabs with a 3px blue top edge: scale word first in 16px, the position as a small signal-coloured pill before it, a dot meter after; it compacts to a single 36px strip on scroll.
Blocks: ruled sections, serif heading, an owner tag and state word at the right; no cards, no fills.
Tables: 13px, hairline rules, head in small caps grey; in-cell headroom and severity drawn in CSS.
Charts: SVG, same kit; summaries read as a sentence under the title, source line below.
Status and verdict: outlined chips, icon + word, signal colour on text and border only.
Provenance as underlines, so figures read inline: cited 11,648 solid blue; calculated 3.29x dotted navy; synthetic 630.9m dashed grey; gap n/a G8; illustrative: dashed outline tag; model-written: a pen tag with a dotted left rule.
Icons: one 16px stroke set; family icons in the module strip; document-type icons in the dock.
