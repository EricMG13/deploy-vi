## Analysis

### Credit view

CZR's Q2 revenue increased to $2,993 million, while issuer Adjusted EBITDA declined to $920 million; its Las Vegas contribution fell even as Regional improved. [C1, C2, C13, C14] MGM and PENN provide relevant gaming comparators, but MGM includes China and excludes the BetMGM venture from MGM Digital, while PENN's Interactive revenue includes a tax gross-up. [C22, C26, C35] More importantly for creditors, CZR's financing-obligation rent sits in interest, whereas the peers' triple-net operating rent affects their reported adjusted measures differently. [C7, C20, C27, C40] These facts prevent a reliable three-company leverage or EBITDA-margin ranking. CZR's lower first-half capex supported cash after capex despite softer EBITDA, a meaningful counterweight, but its recurring rent and debt service still demand cash. [C2, C3, C4, C10, C11] No dated equity values or aligned transaction multiples were delivered; valuation remains an evidence gap, not a recovery conclusion.

### Borrower-versus-peer interpretation

**Operating position.** On reported Q2 results, CZR's Adjusted EBITDA declined from $955 million to $920 million; MGM's consolidated Adjusted EBITDA declined from $647.514 million to $610.387 million, whereas PENN's rose from $236.1 million to $312.6 million. These are three *issuer-defined* measures, not one standardized margin series. [C2, C23, C34] MGM's Las Vegas Strip revenue rose, but its table-game win percentage was 29.6%; CZR's Las Vegas hold was 16.6%, down 4.5 points year over year. [C24, C25, C12] Different casino mixes prevent treating that single-peer, single-quarter spread as a statistical outlier. It does identify a creditor-relevant exposure: if weak CZR hold and leisure demand recur, property earnings and fixed-charge capacity may deteriorate before debt principal declines. CZR's Regional EBITDA improvement is contrary evidence; Windsor's March move into consolidated Regional operations limits organic-growth attribution. [C13, C14, C16]

**Cash flow, debt and definition control.** CP-1's H1 issuer base re-anchors CFO at $675 million and cash property/equipment capex at $335 million, giving $340 million CFO less capex. The comparable prior-year figures give $227 million; reduced capex explains more of the improvement than CFO growth. [C3, C4] At June 30, canonical CZR balance-sheet debt is $11,699 million, from $114 million current plus $11,585 million long-term. The debt note instead reports $11,807 million face and $11,705 million book value; the $108 million face-to-canonical and $6 million note-book-to-canonical differences remain labelled, not silently substituted. Separate long-term financing obligations are $13,157 million. [C5, C6, C7, C9] H1 debt cash interest was $368 million *in addition to* $686 million rent-related financing-obligation cash interest; debt-only interest coverage overstates fixed-cash-charge comfort. [C10, C11] PENN's disclosed lease cash payments and MGM's operating-lease rent cannot be dropped into CZR's debt or interest denominators without reconciliation. [C27, C39, C40]

**Strongest contrary case and monitoring.** CZR's reported $2,928 million cash-plus-revolver liquidity and improved H1 after-capex cash generation argue against an immediate liquidity break. [C3, C4, C8] That case gains force if subsequent CFO remains durable while necessary maintenance and renovations continue; it loses force if lower capex reflects deferral or Las Vegas weakness persists. First observe visitation and table hold, then segment EBITDA and CFO, then revolver use against remaining-2026 debt service, lease payments and planned capex. Those disclosed remaining-year demands are approximately $425 million, $687 million and $310–390 million, respectively; they are not an additive forecast of a funding deficit. [C12, C50, C51, C52] A sustained earnings decline would reduce refinancing flexibility, but contractual covenant headroom and a merger-closing capital structure are not established by this peer exercise.

**Valuation and dates.** Enterprise value would equal dated equity value plus debt, minority interests and preferred equity, less available cash and liquid short-term investments. None of the delivered peer releases supplies the dated equity values needed for a complete trading EV; the pending CZR–Fertitta merger discloses a conditional $31-per-share cash consideration, not a comparable transaction enterprise value or contemporaneous transaction multiple. [C17, C18] Do not treat a proposed deal as completed financing or infer recovery from an unavailable multiple. The May 27 agreement was signed *within* Q2; the disclosed July 13 Alberta launch is a **subsequent event** and is excluded from June metrics. [C17, C21] The customer-deposit/deferred-revenue balance at June 30 was $545 million: prepayments support funding only until wagering settlement or future service is due; weakening new deposits can turn that non-debt float into a liquidity call. [C19] This is distinct from trade payables.
