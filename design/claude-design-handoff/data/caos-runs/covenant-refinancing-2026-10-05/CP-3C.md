Overall vulnerability is Medium, with Low confidence and Restricted QA: consensual refinancing is the conditional base-case working path; coercive LME risk is not demonstrated. The 2024 amendment precedent supports repricing flexibility, not an uptier [C21].

**Downside transmission and counterevidence.** A further weakening in Las Vegas visitation, table-game volume or hold would first reduce property earnings; lower cash conversion could then increase revolver reliance and reduce the capacity to service debt, rent and investment. Q2 Las Vegas Adjusted EBITDA was $410 million versus $469 million a year earlier [C33]. Regional and Digital are partial offsets, but Windsor’s consolidation affects comparability, and the issuer cautions that Adjusted EBITDA omits capital expenditures, interest, taxes, principal and lease payments. The strongest counterargument is management’s statement that current liquidity, committed borrowing availability and operating cash flows should fund obligations for twelve months and beyond, together with reported covenant compliance [C18, C19]. It would prevail if an updated monthly forecast, downside case and facility-access evidence showed adequate headroom through the 2028 refinancing window; without these, confidence and path conviction remain limited.

**Subsequent events.** The Merger Agreement was entered into on May 27, 2026, within the reporting quarter, and is not blended into period-end debt or liquidity [C35]. The Q2 filing states that Alberta mobile sports betting and iGaming launched on July 13, 2026, after the reporting date; no operating or cash-flow contribution is assumed [C36]. No subsequent refinancing or maturity extension after June 30, 2026 is established by the evidence delivered.
