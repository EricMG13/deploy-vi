# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static, self-contained HTML (inline CSS, inline SVG charts, minimal vanilla JS), generated by RBOT's Python renderer; no CDN, no framework, no network at view time. The Report opens from disk. Decided in the Deploy VI design spec (D25, D29); mockups are authored in Claude Design and then become the renderer's template.

## Users

Portfolio managers and the leveraged-finance credit research team at State Street Investment Management (strategies: CLO, Indexed Loans, Indexed HY). PMs read a Report to decide whether and how much to own an issuer or instrument; analysts read it to check, challenge and extend the analysis behind that decision. Both read on a desktop browser on a wide work monitor; laptop width is the minimum. Print/PDF is a secondary export.

## Product Purpose

RBOT (Research BOT) turns an analyst's Source Pack (issuer filings and legal documents, market levels, portfolio positions and limits) into governed credit analysis and one Report per run. The Report must put the call, the key findings and the charts in front of the reader immediately, and let them open any section for the detailed analysis and its evidence. Success: a PM can state the recommendation, the main risk and the price/valuation view within a minute; an analyst can trace any figure to its source in two clicks.

## Positioning

Every figure in the Report is either cited to a page or clause of a document the analyst supplied, or computed by a deterministic script whose inputs are cited; model-written judgement is visibly marked as such; every known limitation is stated before the run and shown in the Report. Charts draw provenance in the mark.

## Operating Context

- One Pathway per run (Full Credit Assessment at Full or Quick depth, Relative Value, Covenant & Refinancing, Distressed & Restructuring, Earnings Update, Portfolio Decision); each opens on its own Front Page. Full Credit Assessment's Front Page is the Credit Snapshot, the house template PMs already use in Excel.
- The Report is shared as a file inside the team, alongside the analyst's own Excel model (RBOT exports a values-only model.xlsx).
- A later run on the same issuer can compare against an earlier Report (Prior Case: "What changed since <date>").

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Credit Snapshot blocks (house template): company profile, analyst recommendation per Strategy, transaction summary (Primary runs only), capital structure with Structuring and SSIM multiples, EBITDA adjustments (Reported → Structuring → SSIM), investment thesis and catalysts, strengths and weaknesses, historical performance, financials and credit metrics, loan and high-yield relative value.
- House scales: Credit Score 1–5 (Strong … Stressed), Valuation V1–V5, Recommendation 1 Strong OW … 5 Strong UW / Avoid / N/A, Credit Direction, Sector Outlook, Portfolio Fit (Fits / Watch / Breaches).
- Chart parity with caos-databricks (D29): Model chart strip, bridges, bullets, ranges, maturity walls, spread curves, risk matrix; inline SVG; a missing value is an n/a gap with its reason; every chart has a table twin.
- Terms follow the Deploy VI glossary (`CONTEXT.md`).

## Brand Commitments

- State Street Investment Management house style as used in the Credit Snapshot Excel template (navy section bars with white bold capitals, dense bordered tables, logo top-right). Colour and type tokens are sampled from State Street's public website (owner, 2026-10-11).
- The official SSIM logo files are in `brand/` (owner-supplied 2026-10-11: horizontal white, stacked black, horizontal blue-on-black, white-on-blue tile; see `brand/README.md`). Only these variants are used; the mark is never redrawn or recoloured. The logo field's electric blue (#0017E5 / #001AFF) and the site's deep navy (#000533) are the house pair.
- Mockups and Reports are private: local files and the owner's private Claude Design project only; never published.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real issuer figures: Caesars Entertainment (CZR) Q2 2026 public filings, TRACE levels and peer (MGM, PENN) releases in the local corpus (`caos-databricks/qualification/czr-2026q2-full-credit-assessment/documents/`), extracted with file and line citations (owner chose real CZR figures for the mockups, 2026-10-11).
- No RBOT run output exists yet: any recommendation, score, thesis or valuation shown in a mockup is illustrative and must be labelled so; it is not a view on Caesars.
- The owner's Credit Snapshot screenshots (`~/Desktop/Screenshot 2026-10-11 at 11.5*.png`) and Sector RV workbook screenshots (`~/Desktop/Market Data/`).

## Product Principles

1. The call first: recommendation, key findings and charts are visible without scrolling or clicking.
2. Depth on demand: every section expands for the full analysis, and every figure opens to its source.
3. Provenance is visible: cited, calculated and model-authored content never look alike.
4. Honest gaps: a missing value is shown as missing with its reason, never as zero or a guess.
5. The house template is home: PMs recognise the Credit Snapshot at a glance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-reachable chart marks with table twins; colour never the only carrier of meaning (provenance and status also in shape, pattern or text); WCAG 2.2 AA contrast.
